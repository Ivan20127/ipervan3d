Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my order?

Choose Local Pickup (free) or Ship Order (+$6) in the Delivery Method box. If you choose shipping, please enter your address in the cart.

How do I pay?

Add your items to the cart, fill in your name and phone number, then continue with Venmo or Zelle. Payment is not automatically verified by this website, so please double-check the amount and recipient before paying.

Do special colors cost extra?

Yes. Specialty colors (multi-color and effect filaments) add $1 per item on items $10 or less, and $2 per item on items over $10. The color dropdown on each product shows the exact extra cost.

Can you make something custom?

Yes! Use the "Request a Custom Print" button to email us your idea, picture, or design. Custom prices depend on the size and complexity of the print.

How do I contact you?

Email us at ivanpervan09@gmail.com.
